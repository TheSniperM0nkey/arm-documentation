AI Usage Policy

This project permits contributors to use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist with code, documentation, testing, and review. AI use is optional. Contributors are responsible for all submitted work, regardless of how it was produced.

Contributor responsibilities

Before submitting a contribution, you must:

  • Understand the changes and be able to explain how they work. Do not submit code you cannot review, maintain, or support.
  • Carefully review AI-generated output for correctness, security, quality, and consistency with the project.
  • Run appropriate tests and address any problems you find.
  • Avoid sharing secrets, personal data, confidential information, or unpublished project material with AI tools unless you are authorized to do so.
  • Ensure that all submitted material may lawfully be contributed and distributed under the project's MIT license. Review any third-party output or material for applicable license and attribution requirements.

AI tools can produce incorrect or unsuitable results; their output is not a substitute for your own judgment and review.

Disclosure

Routine AI assistance does not need to be disclosed. If AI generated a substantial portion of a contribution, note this in the pull request and briefly explain what it generated and how you reviewed and tested it. Do not include confidential prompts or sensitive information.

Project review

AI-assisted contributions follow the same contribution and review process as other submissions. Maintainers may request changes or decline a contribution based on the project's usual standards. Contributors remain responsible for ensuring they have the rights needed to submit their contribution under the MIT license.
